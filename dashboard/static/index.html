About & Methodology
How the prediction is made, what data feeds it, and who built it.

The Weekly Stock Direction Predictor estimates whether Microsoft's (MSFT) closing price will move up, down, or stay same over the next trading week. It uses an XGBoost classifier trained on 20 features: 12 MSFT technical features and 8 peer features from NVDA and AMZN. MSFT inputs include multi-week returns, MACD, RSI, price range, rolling volatility, and volume ratio. Peer inputs capture relative strength and volume momentum against NVDA and AMZN. The dashboard is built for retail investors who want a simple weekly directional signal without wading through code or formulas.
